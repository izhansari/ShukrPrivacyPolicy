# shukr Support

## Get in touch

Email **[hello@better-norms.com](mailto:hello@better-norms.com)**. Say what you expected and what happened; a screenshot helps. Replies usually within a few days.

## Common questions

**The times look wrong.** Check the calculation method and madhab in Settings. Setup picks a method for your country; your local mosque may use another. Asr differs between Hanafi and Shafi'i on purpose.

**No times at all.** shukr needs a place: allow location, or pick a city in Settings.

**Reminders don't arrive.** iOS Settings → Notifications must allow shukr. In the app, each prayer has its own reminder switch. Low Power Mode and Focus can delay them. Opening shukr now and then keeps the week's reminders lined up.

**The Fajr alarm.** On iOS 26.1 and later shukr sets a system alarm itself. On iOS 26.0 it uses a Shortcut, which the app installs for you.

**The qibla seems off.** The map's line is worked out from your position and is right even when the compass isn't. If the compass arrow drifts, move the phone in a figure of eight, away from magnets and cases with magnets.

**Travelling.** With Always location your times, reminders and widgets follow you. With While Using, open shukr once at the new place.

## Your data

Everything shukr keeps (prayers, zikr, notes, memos, photos) stays on your device. There is no account and no server. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md). To delete your data, delete the app.
