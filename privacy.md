# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 30, 2026

shukr is built and operated by **Izhan Ansari** ("we", "us"), United States. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use shukr on iPhone and Apple Watch.

## TL;DR

**shukr collects nothing about you.** No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads. What you do in shukr stays on your device and in your own backups. We never see it.

## What shukr keeps, and where

Your prayer records, scores and streaks, the places you prayed, your prayer photos, your zikr sessions and tasks, your azkar with their notes, voice memos and photos, the days you set as excused, the giving you log, and your settings are stored on your iPhone (and, for what the Watch app needs, on your Apple Watch). They leave it only in your own backups, or in a copy you make yourself:

- **Your iPhone's own backup.** They are included in your device's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on; that backup is Apple's and yours, not ours.
- **shukr's iCloud Backup** (off unless you turn it on, in Settings → Your Data). Once a day shukr saves a copy of all of it, photos and voice memos included, to your own iCloud Drive. It stays in your iCloud account. You can remove it in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → shukr.
- **Export.** A copy is made only when you tap Export, and goes only where you send it.

We cannot see any of it.

## Location

With your permission, shukr uses your location on your device to work out prayer times and the qibla, to pin each prayer where you prayed it, to find mosques near you and, if you turn it on, to show a dua when you arrive at your masjid. With "Always" permission, prayer times and reminders follow you when you travel, even when the app is closed. You can instead pick a city and give no location at all.

Your location is never sent to us. For the city name, the map, mosque search and directions, shukr uses Apple's own location and maps services (Core Location and MapKit), which are covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Opening directions in Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze hands the destination to that app.

## Microphone and camera

Only when you take a prayer photo after marking a prayer, or record a voice memo or take a photo for one of your azkar. The photos and recordings are stored on your device and in your own backups (above), and nowhere else.

## Notifications and alarms

Prayer reminders, zikr reminders and the Fajr alarm are scheduled on your device by the app. No push server is involved.

## Apple Watch

The Watch app and the iPhone app exchange prayer marks, zikr sessions and settings directly between your devices through Apple's Watch Connectivity. Nothing passes through us.

## Third parties

shukr does not include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs. The only third-party code is an open-source prayer-time calculation library (Adhan), which runs entirely on your device. No data is sold or shared, and there is no tracking across apps or websites.

## Children's privacy

shukr collects no information at all. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct, port, restrict or delete. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on your devices (and from your Watch when its app is removed). Copies made by shukr's iCloud Backup stay in your iCloud Drive until you remove them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top and post the new version at the same URL before that release ships.
